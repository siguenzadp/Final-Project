Discover Our Purpose

What is Sikapera?

Sikapera is an educational website designed to help Filipinos understand the basics of personal finance.

The website provides beginner-friendly lessons about budgeting, saving money, responsible spending, and debt management.

Sikapera aims to make financial education simple, accessible, and practical for everyday life.

Our Mission

Our mission is to promote financial literacy by providing accessible and easy-to-understand educational resources that encourage responsible financial decision-making.

Our Vision

Our vision is to help build a financially informed Filipino community where individuals develop healthy money habits and work toward financial stability.

Our Objectives

Sikapera aims to help users improve their financial knowledge through the following objectives:

  1. Educate users about basic financial concepts.
  2. Promote responsible budgeting and spending habits.
  3. Encourage consistent saving and financial goal-setting.
  4. Provide information about responsible borrowing and debt management.
  5. Make financial education accessible and understandable for beginners.

Who is Sikapera For?

Sikapera is designed for Filipinos who want to develop better money management skills.

Students

Learn how to manage allowances, school expenses, and personal savings.

Young Professionals

Understand how to budget salaries, control expenses, and prepare for future goals.

Freelancers

Discover ways to manage irregular income and develop consistent saving habits.

Financial Beginners

Explore financial concepts through simple explanations and practical examples.

Why Financial Literacy Matters

Financial literacy refers to the knowledge and skills needed to make informed decisions about managing money.

Understanding personal finance can help individuals plan expenses, manage financial obligations, and prepare for unexpected situations.

Through financial education, individuals can become more confident in making everyday money decisions.

Supporting Sustainable Development Goal 4

Quality Education

Sikapera supports the broader objectives of United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4, which promotes inclusive and equitable quality education and lifelong learning opportunities.

The website contributes to this educational goal by making financial literacy information accessible to Filipino students, young adults, and beginners.

Connection to SDG Target 4.4

SDG Target 4.4 focuses on increasing the number of young people and adults with relevant skills for employment, decent jobs, and entrepreneurship.

Sikapera complements this goal by providing practical financial education that helps users develop budgeting, saving, and responsible money management skills.
