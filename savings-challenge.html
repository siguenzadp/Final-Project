What is a Savings Challenge?

A savings challenge is a financial activity that encourages individuals to set aside a certain amount of money regularly over a specific period.

It helps people practice saving consistently while monitoring their progress toward a financial goal.

Sikapera introduces a beginner-friendly savings challenge designed for Filipino students and young adults.

Why Try a Savings Challenge?

Build Saving Habits

Saving regularly encourages individuals to develop financial discipline and responsible money management habits.

Practice Financial Planning

A savings challenge helps individuals learn how to allocate part of their available money toward future goals.

Monitor Financial Progress

Tracking savings allows individuals to see how small contributions can accumulate over time.

Develop Financial Confidence

Reaching small savings goals may encourage individuals to continue saving for larger financial objectives.

The ₱100 Weekly Savings Challenge

The Sikapera savings challenge encourages participants to set aside ₱100 each week for 12 consecutive weeks.

By completing the challenge, participants can accumulate ₱1,200 in savings without including any interest earned.

The challenge is only an example. Participants may adjust the weekly amount based on their available income and essential expenses.

Step 1: Set Your Savings Goal

Decide how much money you want to save during the 12-week period and determine whether the amount is realistic.

Step 2: Choose Your Weekly Amount

Start with ₱100 per week or select a smaller amount that fits your allowance, income, and financial responsibilities.

Step 3: Set Aside Your Savings

Save the planned amount each week and keep it separate from money intended for everyday expenses.

Step 4: Record Your Progress

Use a notebook or simple savings tracker to record the amount you have saved each week.

Step 5: Review Your Achievement

At the end of 12 weeks, calculate your total savings and consider setting another realistic savings goal.

Tips for Completing the Savings Challenge

Start Small

Begin with an amount that is manageable based on your available money.

Save Consistently

Try setting aside your savings on the same day each week to develop a routine.

Avoid Unnecessary Purchases

Identify optional expenses that you can reduce without affecting your essential needs.

Keep Savings Separate

Store your savings separately from everyday spending money to help avoid accidental use.

Monitor Your Progress

Record your contributions to see how much you have saved and how close you are to your goal.

Stay Flexible

If unexpected expenses arise, adjust your savings amount instead of neglecting important financial responsibilities.
