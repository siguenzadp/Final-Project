What is a Financial Glossary?

A financial glossary is a collection of words and definitions related to money management and personal finance.

Understanding financial terms can help individuals make informed decisions about spending, saving, borrowing, and planning for the future.

Why Learn Financial Terms?

Improve Financial Knowledge

Understand important concepts commonly used in financial discussions and educational resources.

Make Informed Decisions

Recognize financial terminology when comparing financial products, services, and money management options.

Build Financial Confidence

Become more comfortable learning about personal finance and applying financial concepts in everyday life.

Common Financial Terms and Definitions

Explore the following financial terms arranged alphabetically for easier learning.

Allowance

Money regularly given to someone, often by parents or guardians, for daily expenses or personal needs.

Annual Percentage Rate (APR)

A yearly rate used to describe the cost of borrowing money, which may include certain fees depending on the financial product and applicable rules.

Budget

A financial plan that shows how income will be divided among expenses, savings, and other financial responsibilities.

Compound Interest

Interest calculated on the original amount of money and on interest previously added to that amount.

Credit

The ability to borrow money or receive goods and services with an agreement to pay later.

Debt

Money that a person or organization owes and is expected to repay under agreed terms.

Emergency Fund

Money saved specifically to help cover unexpected and necessary expenses or financial emergencies.

Expense

Money spent on goods, services, bills, and other financial obligations.

Financial Goal

A specific money-related objective that a person plans to achieve within a certain period.

Income

Money received from sources such as employment, business activities, allowances, or other earnings.

Interest

Money paid for borrowing funds or money earned from certain savings and financial products.

Interest Rate

A percentage used to calculate interest on borrowed money or certain savings balances over a specified period.

Needs

Essential goods and services required for daily living, such as food, housing, transportation, and necessary education expenses.

Principal

The original amount of money borrowed or deposited before interest is added.

Savings

Money set aside instead of being spent immediately, usually for future needs or financial goals.

Simple Interest

Interest calculated only on the original principal amount over a specified period.

Wants

Non-essential goods and services that provide enjoyment, comfort, or convenience.

Tips for Understanding Financial Terms

  • Start with basic terms such as income, expenses, budget, and savings.
  • Connect each financial term to real-life situations.
  • Review unfamiliar words whenever you encounter them.
  • Use examples to understand how financial concepts work.
  • Explore trusted educational resources to improve your understanding.
