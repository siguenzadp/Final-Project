About Our References

Sikapera aims to provide beginner-friendly financial education supported by credible and accessible information sources.

This page presents the educational references, website design guidelines, and media attributions associated with the project.

The reference list follows APA 7th edition conventions and will be updated as additional sources are incorporated into the website.

Sustainable Development Goal References

Sikapera is aligned with the broader educational objectives of United Nations Sustainable Development Goal 4: Quality Education.

  • United Nations Department of Economic and Social Affairs. (n.d.). Goal 4: Ensure inclusive and equitable quality education and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all. https://sdgs.un.org/goals/goal4

SDG 4 promotes inclusive education and lifelong learning opportunities. Sikapera complements these objectives by providing beginner-friendly financial literacy lessons.

Media Attribution

Sikapera will include educational images and videos to make financial concepts easier to understand.

Educational Content and Accuracy

Sikapera presents financial concepts through simplified explanations, illustrative examples, and general educational guidance.

Sample budgets, savings calculations, and loan examples are intended to support learning and may not represent individual financial situations.

Users should verify relevant information through official institutions when making important financial decisions.

Disclaimer

Sikapera is an educational website created for academic purposes.

Information presented on this website is intended for general financial education and should not be considered personalized financial advice.
