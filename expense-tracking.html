What is Expense Tracking?

Expense tracking is the process of recording how much money you spend and what you spend it on.

Reviewing your expenses can help you identify spending patterns and understand whether you are following your budget.

Why Track Your Expenses?

Understand Your Spending

See which purchases and expense categories use most of your money.

Make Better Decisions

Recognize unnecessary spending and decide where adjustments may be helpful.

Support Your Savings Goals

Find opportunities to set aside money for future needs and personal goals.

How to Track Your Expenses

Step 1: Choose a Recording Method

Use a notebook, a spreadsheet, or another method that makes recording expenses easy for you.

Step 2: Record Every Purchase

Write down the date, item or purpose, category, and amount of each expense.

Step 3: Group Similar Expenses

Organize purchases into categories such as food, transportation, school needs, bills, and entertainment.

Step 4: Add Your Expenses

Calculate how much you spent in each category and the total amount spent during the week or month.

Step 5: Compare Spending with Your Budget

Review your totals and consider whether your planned budget needs to change.

Simple Ways to Record Expenses

Notebook

Use pen and paper to write down your purchases each day.

Spreadsheet

Organize expenses into rows and columns to make totals easier to review.

Receipts and Transaction Records

Review receipts or available transaction histories to help remember purchases you may have missed.

Tips for Better Expense Tracking

  • Record expenses as soon as possible so you do not forget them.
  • Include small purchases because they can add up over time.
  • Use consistent categories when reviewing your records.
  • Review your totals regularly rather than waiting until the end of the month.
  • Keep personal financial records private and secure.
