What is budgeting?

Budgeting is the process of planning how you will spend and save your money based on your available income.

A budget helps you track expenses, avoid unnecessary spending, and prepare for future financial needs.

Why is Budgeting Important?

Control Spending

Understand where your money goes and avoid unnecessary purchases.

Build Savings

Set aside money regularly for future goals and emergencies.

Avoid Unnecessary Debt

Plan your expenses to reduce unnecessary borrowing.

Acieve Goals

Manage your finances to work toward your personal goals.

How to Create a Budget

Step 1: Identify Your Income

Determine how much money you receive from your salary, allowance, or other sources of income.

Step 2: List Your Expenses

Write down your regular expenses such as food, transportation, rent, utilities, and school needs.

Step 3: Separate Needs and Wants

Identify which expenses are necessary and which are optional.

Step 4: Set Your Savings Goal

Decide how much money you can realistically save each month.

Monitor your spendinga and adjust your budget when necessary.

Needs vs. Wants

Understanding the difference between needs and wants can help you make better spending decisions.

Needs

Essential expenses required for daily living.

  • Food and groceries
  • Housing and rent
  • Utilities
  • Transportation
  • Education expenses

Wants

Non-essential expenses that improve comfort or enjoyment.

  • Online shopping
  • Entertainment subscriptions
  • Dining out
  • Gaming purchases
  • Luxury items
